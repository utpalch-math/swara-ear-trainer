# Swara Ear Trainer

An offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help learners of Hindustani Classical Music develop relative pitch identification against a continuous Tanpura drone.

**Live App:** [https://utpalch-math.github.io/swara-ear-trainer/](https://utpalch-math.github.io/swara-ear-trainer/)

---

### Purpose
In Indian classical music, notes (*swaras*) gain meaning only in relation to the fundamental drone (*Adhara Shadja* / Sa) and the framework of a parent scale (*Thaat*). This app trains the ear to perceive and name swaras within their authentic modal context rather than as isolated equal-tempered frequencies.

---

### Features & How to Use

1. **Select Tonic and Thaat:**  
   Choose your base pitch (Sa) and any of the ten Bhatkhande Thaats (Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Kafi, Asavari, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Todi, Purvi, or Marwa).

2. **Tanpura Drone & Melodic Path:**  
   * Start a continuous tanpura loop with traditional stroke patterns.
   * Listen to the ascending (**Aroh**) and descending (**Avroh**) paths to internalize the modal character of the selected Thaat.

3. **Ear-Training Challenges:**  
   * Choose to test yourself on sequences of **1, 2, 3, or 4 notes**.
   * Click **Generate Challenge** to hear random swaras played within that Thaat.
   * You have three attempts to correctly identify the sequence using the swara buttons.

4. **Free Exploration:**  
   Freely tap any swara button to audition notes within the chosen Thaat across the middle octave.

---

### Installation (Runs Offline)

* **Mobile (Android / iPhone):** Open the link in Chrome or Safari, tap the browser menu (or Share button), and select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
* **Laptop / Desktop:** Open in Chrome or Edge and click the **Install** icon in the address bar.

Once loaded, the app requires no app store, consumes virtually no storage, and runs completely offline.
